Privacy Policy

Summary

Your personal health information is protected under Namibian law. Health service providers must ensure confidentiality, only sharing your information with consent or legal obligation. You have the right to access your medical records and request corrections.

1. Information We Collect

We collect data to provide quality health services. This may include:

  • Personal Details: Name, surname, date of birth, gender, ID/Passport number
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, work telephone
  • Professional Information: Profession, institution employed, medical qualifications
  • Health Information: Medical history, treatment records, allergies, medications (for patients)
  • Account Information: Email, encrypted password, login timestamps

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage health services
  • To communicate with you about appointments, results, and health updates
  • To maintain accurate medical records
  • To comply with legal obligations (Namibian health regulations)
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • For authentication and account security

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely using Supabase (Postgres) with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Access is controlled via Supabase Authentication — users only access their own data
  • Medical staff only access patient data for patients under their care
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Data hosted in Google Cloud regions with enterprise-grade security

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With your explicit consent
  • With healthcare providers directly involved in your care
  • When required by Namibian law or court order
  • For public health reporting (anonymized/aggregated)
  • With service providers under strict data processing agreements

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Objection: Object to processing for direct marketing

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Help & Contact page.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while account is active, deleted 2 years after account closure
  • Medical records: Retained per Namibian health regulations (minimum 10 years for adults)
  • Login/logs: Retained for 12 months for security auditing

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising networks.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16. If we learn we've collected data from a child without parental consent, we'll delete it immediately.

9. International Transfers

Data may be processed in Supabase cloud regions outside Namibia. Providers apply industry-standard safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email or prominent notice. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us
